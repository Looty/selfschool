After this lesson you can say what a cup of tea needs and why the water temperature matters.

## What you need

Tea needs three things: leaves, hot water and time. [1]

## Water temperature

Black tea is usually brewed with water just off the boil, while green tea prefers cooler water so it does not turn bitter. [1]

:::key-takeaways
- Leaves, hot water and time.
- Black tea likes hotter water than green tea.
:::
