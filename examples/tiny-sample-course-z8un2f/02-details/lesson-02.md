After this lesson you can order the steps of making tea and match teas to their steeping time.

## Steps

Boil the water, warm the cup, add the leaves, pour, wait, then remove the leaves. [1]

## Steeping time

Green tea steeps for about two minutes, black tea for about four. [1]

:::low-confidence
Steeping times vary with the tea and personal taste. Treat these as a starting point. [1]
:::
