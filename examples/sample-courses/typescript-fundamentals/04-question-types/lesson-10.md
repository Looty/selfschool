Connect each item on the left to its partner on the right. Pick one, then pick the other, or drag from the left to the right. A connected pair shares a colour and a line.

Pressing a connected item cuts its line. The right-hand side may hold extra items that belong to nothing, so not every item needs a partner. After checking, a wrong pairing also shows where it should have gone.
