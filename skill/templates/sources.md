# Sources

Numbers are stable: lessons and questions cite them as `[n]`. Never renumber; append new sources at the end.
Only list sources that were actually read during research. Never invent a URL.

1. [Source title](https://example.com/page) · publisher · accessed YYYY-MM-DD · reliability: high · read: full
   - Notes: what this source is used for (sections, tables and figures you took facts from); version or date it applies to.
2. [Another source](https://example.com/other) · publisher · accessed YYYY-MM-DD · reliability: medium · read: abstract only
   - Notes: why reliability is not high (blog, secondary, single author); what the unread part might contain. Replace it with the primary document when you can.

`read:` is `full`, `partly (sections 1 to 3)` or `abstract only`. A lesson never claims more than that level supports.
